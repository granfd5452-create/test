# Product Idea #1: Daily Shift Handover System

## 1) Form Fields to Include

Design this as a printable + fillable digital form with one-page **Shift Summary** and optional second-page **Issue Log**.

### A. Header / Context
- **Business / Team Name**
- **Department / Unit** (e.g., Front Desk, Warehouse, Kitchen Line 2)
- **Date**
- **Shift Type** (Morning / Afternoon / Night / Custom)
- **Shift Start–End Time**
- **Outgoing Lead Name**
- **Incoming Lead Name**
- **Handover Time Completed**

### B. Staffing Snapshot
- **Planned Headcount**
- **Actual Headcount**
- **Absences / No-shows**
- **Late Arrivals / Early Leaves**
- **Role Coverage Gaps**
- **Temp / Agency Staff on Shift**

### C. Operations Status (RAG format)
Use status chips: **Green (on track), Amber (watch), Red (urgent)**.
- **Core Process 1 Status + Note**
- **Core Process 2 Status + Note**
- **Core Process 3 Status + Note**
- **Bottlenecks Encountered**
- **Workarounds Used**

### D. KPIs / Throughput
- **Target Metric(s)** (units/hour, tickets resolved, patients seen, etc.)
- **Actual Performance**
- **Variance (+/-)**
- **Top Reason for Variance**

### E. Tasks Completed This Shift
- **Task / Activity**
- **Owner**
- **Completion Time**
- **Outcome / Notes**

### F. Open Items for Next Shift
- **Priority (High / Med / Low)**
- **Open Task Description**
- **Owner / Team**
- **Due By**
- **Dependencies / Blockers**

### G. Incidents / Risks / Safety
- **Incident Type** (Safety, Service, Quality, Compliance, Equipment)
- **Description**
- **Impact Level**
- **Immediate Action Taken**
- **Escalated To**
- **Follow-up Required (Y/N)**

### H. Equipment / System Health
- **Equipment/System Name**
- **Status (Operational / Degraded / Down)**
- **Ticket # / Case #**
- **ETA to Resolution**

### I. Inventory / Materials (if relevant)
- **Critical Item Name**
- **Current Stock**
- **Reorder Threshold**
- **Risk of Stockout (Y/N)**
- **Action Taken**

### J. Communications Log
- **Stakeholder Contacted**
- **Message Summary**
- **Time Sent**
- **Response / Pending**

### K. Sign-off
- **Outgoing Lead Signature / Initials**
- **Incoming Lead Signature / Initials**
- **Manager Review (optional)**

## 2) Dashboard Structure (for Notion/Sheets/Airtable template)

Create a dashboard that sits above form submissions and answers one question fast: **“What does the next shift need to know right now?”**

## A. Layout

### Top Row: KPI Tiles
1. **Open High-Priority Items**
2. **Unresolved Incidents**
3. **Shifts Logged This Week**
4. **On-time Handovers %**

### Middle Left: “Action for Next Shift” Board
Kanban columns:
- **Urgent Now**
- **Do This Shift**
- **Waiting / Blocked**
- **Done**

Card fields:
- Task
- Priority
- Owner
- Due time
- Related incident/equipment

### Middle Right: Incident & Risk Panel
- Filtered table of open incidents
- Quick tags: Safety / Quality / Service / Compliance
- Aging indicator (hours open)

### Bottom Left: Operations Trend Section
- 7-day trend chart of key KPI(s)
- Missed-target count by shift
- Most common bottleneck category

### Bottom Right: Handover Compliance
- Who submitted late/missing handovers
- Average handover completion time
- Team/department comparison

## B. Views to Include
- **Manager View**: Cross-team summary + trends
- **Shift Lead View**: Only today’s active handover items
- **Incident View**: Only risk/safety/quality records
- **Archive View**: Searchable log by date/department

## C. Filters / Controls
- Date range
- Department
- Shift type
- Priority level
- Incident status

## D. Automation Ideas
- Auto-highlight overdue open items (red)
- Auto-calculate variance from KPI target
- Auto-reminder for missing shift handover submission
- Auto-archive completed tasks after X days

## 3) Product Packaging for Etsy + Gumroad

Sell as a **template bundle** so buyers get immediate value.

## A. Deliverables Bundle
- 1x **Printable PDF** (A4 + US Letter)
- 1x **Fillable PDF**
- 1x **Google Sheets dashboard template**
- 1x **Notion version** (optional premium tier)
- 1x **Quick-start guide** (PDF with setup in <15 min)
- 1x **Field glossary** (what each section means)

## B. Product Tiers

### Tier 1: Starter (Printable Only)
- Printable + fillable handover forms
- Best for teams transitioning from paper/manual process

### Tier 2: Pro (Digital Dashboard)
- Everything in Starter
- Sheets dashboard with tracking views + KPI tiles
- Best for small operations teams

### Tier 3: Ops Pack (Premium)
- Everything in Pro
- Notion version
- Multi-department trackers + extra incident log templates
- Best for managers running multiple teams/sites

## 4) Pricing Strategy

Use value-based pricing anchored to time saved and incident reduction.

## Recommended Price Points (USD)
- **Starter**: $9–$15
- **Pro**: $19–$35
- **Ops Pack**: $39–$69

## Launch Tactic
- Launch at lower end for first 20–50 sales to build reviews.
- Raise price by ~20–30% after social proof.

## Discount Strategy
- 20% launch coupon (first 2 weeks)
- Bundle discount if buyer purchases related operations templates
- Seasonal promo (end-of-quarter operational cleanup)
